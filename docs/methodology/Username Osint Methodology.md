Document:- "Username OSINT Investigation Methodology v1.1.md

SECTION 1:- Trigger Points
a) ----  When Investigator has only a Username:-  Start by searching the username across all major platforms one by one. Since you have no other context, treat every platform as a possible match. Begin with Instagram, Twitter, and Telegram. If found, note all visible public data and move to Section 2  

b) ----  When Investigator has Username + Platform:-  Go directly to that platform first and pull everything available. Once done, take the same username and search all other platforms as well. Do not stop at just the confirmed platform.

c) ----  When Username is found during other Invetigation:-  Note it down immediately but do not shift focus from the main investigation. Come back to this username once the primary lead has enough progress. Even small usernames found in comments, bios, or tags can turn into strong leads later.

d) ----  Priority Level For Each Triggers:-  
              i) ----  Only Username, no Other Info:- P0 - Start immediately
              ii) ----  Username + Platform Confirm:- p0 - Direct Entry Point
              iii) ----  Username Found During Another Case:- p1 - Follow Up after main Thread 
              iv) ----  Username Found but case is not Urgent:- P2- Queue for later

STEP 2 ----  Content Analysis

           1) ----  Instagram:-  Go through last 12 posts
                                 Note captions — recurring themes, tone, language used
                                 Save all location tags
                                 List all hashtags used, pull top 10 separately
                                 Check tagged photos — who is tagging this person and how often
                                 Note any accounts mentioned in captions
                                 See post Comments 
                                 Analyse Timing and dates 
                                 Analyse their Stories and Highlights
                                 
           2) ----  Twitter:-     Go through last 50 tweets
                                 Note main topics they post about
                                 Check language and tone — formal, casual, Hinglish etc
                                 Look at timestamp patterns to figure out active time of day and timezone
                                 Note accounts they reply to or mention most
                                 List most used hashtags
                                 Check retweets — what kind of content they amplify and whose
      
          3) ----  Telegram:-    Go through public posts or channel messages
                                 Note topics, language, posting frequency
                                 Check forwarded messages and trace back to original source
                                 Note any external links shared — websites, other social handles
                                 Look at posting time patterns
                                 
          4) ----  Linkedin:-    Go through posts and articles published
                                 Note topics — professional interests, opinions, industry focus
                                 Check comments and reactions on their posts — who engages with them
                                 Look at what they share or repost and from which accounts
                                 
                        
STEP 3 ----  Relationship Mapping

          1) ----  Core Connection:-  Who tags this person regularly across platforms
                                      Who this person tags regularly
                                      Accounts that comment most frequently on their posts
                                      Accounts they reply to most on Twitter/Telegram
                                      Mutual followers — look for accounts that appear across multiple people already in the investigation
                                      
          2) ----  Network Patterns:-  Identify close circle vs casual connections — close circle will show up repeatedly across tagging, comments, and mentions
                                       Check if same accounts appear across multiple platforms — someone who tags them on Instagram and also appears in their Twitter replies is a stronger connection
                                       Look for group affiliations — which WhatsApp/Telegram groups are they part of, which LinkedIn groups, which Twitter communities
                                       Check event-based connections — people who tagged them or got tagged at the same location or event
                                
          3) ----  For Channels/Groups:-  Who are the admins
                                          Who are the most active members
                                          Who forwards their content the most
                                          Who likes or react most on their content or post
                                          
          4) ----  On Linkedin:-        Recommendations received — who wrote them and what's their relationship
                                        Recommendations given — who they vouched for
                                        Mutual connections with other subjects already in the investigation
                                        
          5) ----  Additional Ideas:-  If you find a phone number, check it on Truecaller — the name registered and the tags others have given it can reveal a lot
                                       If you find an email, check which platforms it's registered on via breach databases or password reset flows
                                       Map out a simple connection diagram if there are more than 3-4 key people involved — helps spot clusters and central nodes quickly
                                       
                                       
STEP 3 ----  Cross Platform Discovery

          1) ----  Username Matching:-  i)  Search exact username on all platforms first
                                        ii)  Then try common variations:
                                            Added numbers at the end (rahul99, rahul123)
                                            Platform suffixes (_ig, _tw, _tg, _yt)
                                            Underscores added or removed
                                            Dots instead of underscores (rahul.kumar vs rahul_kumar)
                                            Leet speak swaps (0 for o, 3 for e, 1 for i)
                                            Shortened versions of the name
                                            Name + city or profession (rahul_delhi, rahul_dev)
                                        iii)  If Something found, note each one and don't assume it's the same person yet, verify with other signals
                                        
          2) ----  Profile Picture:-    i)  Download the profile picture from every platform found
                                        ii)  Run through Google Images, Yandex, and TinEye
                                        ii)  Yandex works best for Indian faces
                                        iv)  If same image found elsewhere note the source URL and platform
                                        v)  Even if cropped or slightly filtered, perceptual hash comparison should catch it
                                        vi)  If heavily edited, use face matching as a backup
                                        
          3) ----  Bio Text:-           i)  Copy bio text from every platform
                                        ii)  Compare manually for similar phrasing, same lines copied, or same keywords
                                        iii)  Even partial matches count — people often shorten their bio for some platforms
                                        iv)  Check for same phone number or email across bios
                                         v)  Check for same tagline or quote used consistently
                                         
          4) ----  Connected Accounts:- i)  If bio has a Linktree or similar aggregator link, open it and note every platform linked
                                        ii)  Follow any direct social media links found in bios
                                        iii)  Check if any posts contain links to their other accounts
                                        
          5) ----  Google Dorkings:-    i)  site:instagram.com "username"
                                            site:twitter.com "username"
                                            site:github.com "username"
                                            "username" + "platform name"
                                            "username" + phone number or email if you have it
                                            "full name" + city + profession
                                            This can surface profiles on platforms you wouldn't normally check
                                            
          6) ----  Additional Sources:-  i)  Check username on Namechk or similar tools to see which platforms it's registered on
                                         ii)  Search the username on Google as-is, sometimes old forum posts, gaming profiles, or blog comments show up
                                         iii)  Check Internet Archive / Wayback Machine for old versions of profiles that may have had more info
                                         iv)  If adult platform detection is needed, run the username through dorking with relevant site filters and flag separately — do not mix this into the main correlation score


SECTION 3 ----  Correlation Rules (Weighted Scoring):----  Rule 1: Exact username match = 30 points
                                                           Rule 2: Same profile picture = 25 points
                                                           Rule 3: Same phone number in bio = 30 points
                                                           Rule 4: Same email in bio = 30 points
                                                           Rule 5: Same website or domain in bio = 30 points
                                                           Rule 6: Username pattern match = 20 points
                                                           Rule 7: Bio keyword similarity above 80% = 15 points
                                                           Rule 8: Cross-tagging same accounts = 15 points
                                                           Rule 9: Same location mentioned = 10 points
                                                           Rule 10: Same writing style = 10 points
                                                           Rule 11: Same posting time patterns = 12 points
                                                           Rule 12: Same hashtag clusters = 10 points
                                                           Rule 13: Same pinned post or featured content = 10 points
                                                           Rule 14: Bio link graph — same Linktree or aggregator link = 20 points
                                                           Rule 15: Reverse image search match on unrelated platform = 18 points
                                        
                                        
Confidence Scoring:----

80 and above: Very High — near certain same person, proceed with high confidence
55 to 79: High — likely same person, document evidence and verify once before acting
30 to 54: Medium — probable same person, flag for manual review and look for more signals
Below 30: Low — not enough evidence, collect more data or drop the lead


SECTION 4 ----  Indian Platform Specific:----
                                               1) Koo:---  i)  Check for exact username and common variations
                                                           ii)  Popular with politicians, journalists, and public figures
                                                           iii)  Most content is in Hindi and regional languages
                                                           iv)  API access is very limited, check manually through app or website
                                                            v)  Username patterns often match Twitter since many users migrated from there
                                                            
                                               2) Sharechat:---  i) Mobile-first, mostly tier 2 and tier 3 city users
                                                                 ii)  Content is heavily regional language based
                                                                 iii)  Some content is region-locked, may need local IP or SIM to access
                                                                 iv)  Usernames sometimes in regional script, check for translated or romanized versions of the name
                                                                 v)  Good source for regional identity confirmation
                                                                 
                                               3) Moj:--- i)  Short video platform, similar to TikTok
                                                          ii)  Username to phone number correlation sometimes possible since many sign up with mobile number
                                                          iii)  Profile pictures and faces in videos useful for cross-platform matching
                                                          iv)  Popular in smaller cities and towns

                                               4) Local platforms:---  i)  Matrimonial sites (Shaadi.com, Jeevansaathi): useful for name, photo, and location cross-referencing
                                                                       ii)  OLX and Quikr: listings often have phone numbers and names, older listings stay up even after social accounts are deleted, search by phone or name if you have it
                                                                       iii)  Truecaller: if you have a phone number, check the registered name and any tags others have given it
                                                                       iv)  Just Dial: business listings sometimes tied to personal numbers, useful if subject runs a small business
