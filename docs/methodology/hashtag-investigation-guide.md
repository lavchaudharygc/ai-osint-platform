Hashtag Investigation Guide

Important Hashtags for Investigation

                  1)  Personal Branded Hashtags:----  eg:- #lavchaudharygc
                         i)  Unique to the person, strongest identity signal
                         ii)  If same hashtag found on two different platform accounts it is near certain same person
                         iii)  Check if this hashtag appears in other people's posts as well — those people are close associates
                         iv)  Search this hashtag across Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube to find all content linked to this identity 
                  
                  2)  Location Hashtags:----  eg:- #delhi, #mumbai, #Dwarka, #Cannaught_place
                         i)  Broad city level hashtags like #delhi are weak on their own
                         ii)  Specific locality or neighbourhood hashtags like #dwarkasector12 or #lajpatnagar are much stronger
                         iii)  Cross reference with location tags in posts to verify if claimed location matches actual posting location
                         iv)  Repeated use of same locality hashtag over time confirms the person lives or works in that area
                          
                  3)  Event Hashtags:----  eg:- #iitdelhi2019, #defcon2023
                         i)  Useful for timeline reconstruction — if person used an event hashtag, they were likely present at that event
                         ii)  Cross reference with other attendees using the same hashtag to find real world associates
                         iii)  Conference and seminar hashtags are especially useful for cyber and professional investigations
                         iv)  Check the date range of event hashtag usage to build a movement timeline
                         
                  4)  Community Hashtags:----  eg:- #ethicalhacking, #bugbounty, #Osint, #CyberSecurity
                         i)  Reveals professional interests and community affiliations
                         ii)  If subject uses niche community hashtags, search those hashtags to find their circle
                         iii)  Recurring community hashtags across platforms confirm consistent identity and interest area
                         iv)  Useful for finding alternate accounts — same niche hashtags on an unknown account may link back to subject
                         
                  5)  Assosiation Hashtags:----  eg:- #teamlav, #lavchaudharygccrew
                         i)  Group or crew hashtags used by a tight circle
                         ii)  If multiple accounts use the same association hashtag, map all of them
                         iii)  These are strong signals for identifying close real world connections
                         
                         
                         
What We Can Extract

                 From Personal Branded Hashtags:----    i)  All posts across platforms where this hashtag was used
                                                        ii)  List of other accounts that used this hashtag — potential associates or followers
                                                        iii)  Timeline of when this hashtag was first used and how frequently
                                                        iv)  Platforms where this hashtag exists — cross platform identity confirmation
                                                        
                 From Location Hashtags:----            i)  Current or frequent locations of the subject
                                                        ii)  Movement patterns over time if location hashtags change across posts
                                                        iii)  Verification of claimed location in bio versus actual posting location
                                                        iv)  Specific areas within a city — narrows down home, office, or frequently visited spots
                                                        
                 From Even Hashtags:----                i)  Events attended — physical presence confirmed
                                                        ii)  Timeline of activities and travel
                                                        iii)  Real world connections made at those events
                                                        iv)  Professional interests based on type of events attended
                                                        
                 From Community Hashtags:----           i)  Interest areas and professional domain
                                                        ii)  Community circle — other accounts active in the same niche
                                                        iii)  Skill level indication — beginner communities vs advanced professional communities
                                                        iv)  Online groups or forums the subject is part of
                                                        
                 From Hashtags Frequecy Analysis:----   i)  Top 10 most used hashtags reveal primary interests
                                                        ii) Sudden change in hashtag patterns can indicate a change in location, job, or identity
                                                        iii)  Hashtags used only once or twice are often more personal and specific than frequently used ones
                                                        
                                                        
        
How to Search Hashtags

                 On Instagram:----  i)  Search the hashtag directly in the app or web
                                    ii)  Check recent posts and top posts separately
                                    iii)  Look for the subject's posts under that hashtag
                                    iv)  Note other accounts appearing repeatedly under the same hashtag

                 On Twitter:----    i)  Search hashtag in Twitter search
                                    ii)  Filter by latest to see most recent usage
                                    iii)  Filter by people you follow if investigation account follows known associates
                                    iv)  Check who else is using the same niche hashtag

                 On YouTube:----    i)  Search hashtag in YouTube search bar
                                    ii)  Check video descriptions for hashtag usage
                                    iii)  Personal branded hashtags in video descriptions are strong identity signals

Correlation Logic

                 i)  Same unique personal branded hashtag on 2 profiles → +15 points, very strong signal, near certain same person
                 ii)  Same niche community hashtag on 2 profiles → +8 points, same person likely if combined with other signals 
                 iii)  Same location hashtag (specific locality level) → +8 points, location match confirmed
                 iv)  Same location hashtag (broad city level like #delhi) → +3 points, weak signal, do not rely on this alone
                 v)  Same event hashtag on 2 profiles → +10 points, same person attended same event, strong behavioral signal
                 vi)  Same association or crew hashtag → +12 points, tight circle signal, map all accounts using it
                 vii)  Hashtag first used on one platform then appeared on another around same time → +5 points, cross platform activity pattern
                 viii)  Top 10 hashtags overlap more than 60 percent across two profiles → +10 points, same interest fingerprint
